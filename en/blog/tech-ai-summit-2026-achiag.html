The future is agentic: our day at Tech AI Summit 2026

On Tuesday, November 3, we were at Tech AI Summit 2026 with a booth all day, plus a podcast and a workshop alongside ACHIAG. And we were part of a milestone: the official launch of the Chilean Association of Agentic Artificial Intelligence, chaired by our CEO, Sebastián Figueroa.

Nov 4, 2026 Events · Tech AI Summit 4 min read By Hapee Team
The news in brief

ACHIAG, the Chilean Association of Agentic Artificial Intelligence (Asociación Chilena de Inteligencia Artificial Agéntica), was officially launched at Tech AI Summit with the signing and official photo of its Founding Council. Its president and founder is Sebastián Figueroa, CEO of Hapee and Digitals.

A full day at the new Casa Piedra

Tech AI Summit took place at the new Casa Piedra, next to Claro Arena, in San Carlos de Apoquindo, Las Condes (Santiago). Our booth was open all day, and the agenda had two key moments:

All day
Hapee booth, open throughout the event.
11:40 – 13:00
Hapee + ACHIAG podcast and workshop, in front of a backdrop that said it all: “El futuro es agéntico” (the future is agentic), with Hapee on top and “Powered by ACHIAG” below.
14:00 – 14:20
Signing and official photo of ACHIAG’s Founding Council on stage: the association’s official launch.

Why “the future is agentic”

The phrase on the backdrop wasn’t a coincidence. AI is moving from answering questions to getting work done, and that’s what Hapee’s agents do every day: they chat on WhatsApp and by voice, book appointments and follow up on their own, all inside one CRM.

What ACHIAG is

ACHIAG is a private non-profit corporation that promotes the responsible, ethical, safe and trustworthy development of artificial intelligence in Chile, with a focus on agentic AI. It brings the ecosystem together and represents it before society, industry, academia and the State. It works on four fronts:

Ecosystem & networking
Connecting the builders of autonomous agents with the country’s key industries.
Standards & security
Creating Chile’s first “Safe Agent Seal”.
Training & adoption
Teaching boards and management teams how to bring a digital workforce into real operations.
Regulatory representation
Being the sector’s voice in the discussion of the AI law in Chile’s Congress.

Our CEO, Sebastián Figueroa, is its president and founder. You can meet the Founding Council and see the roadmap at achiag.cl (in Spanish).
