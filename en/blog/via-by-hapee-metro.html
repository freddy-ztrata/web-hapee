Vía by Hapee advances to the next phase: we're going for the AI of the Santiago Metro

We presented Vía by Hapee —our AI travel assistant for passengers— at Metro de Santiago's Demo Day 2026, "Transforming the network that moves the city". Today we can share it: the project advanced to the next phase, and soon we'll be competing to become the official AI of Metro de Santiago.

Aug 23, 2026 News · Vía by Hapee 4 min read By Hapee Team

We have news we're especially excited to share: Vía by Hapee advanced to the next phase at Metro de Santiago's Demo Day 2026, the open innovation process that this year was convened under the motto "Transforming the network that moves the city". And what's coming is even bigger: soon we'll be competing to become the official AI of Metro de Santiago.

The news in brief

We presented Vía at Metro's Demo Day 2026, the project passed this stage of the process and with that we're already in at Metro: mentorships and AI development plans alongside the network. The grand winner will be announced at the end of the year — and we're already exploring side projects with Metro.

What Vía is, in one sentence

Vía by Hapee is an AI travel assistant that accompanies Metro passengers in real time, with a special focus on contingency moments: a stopped train, a detour, a service change. Right when support channels collapse and uncertainty spikes, Vía answers on the channel where the passenger already is.

One brain
It's not one agent per station: it's a single engine serving the whole network and scaling to future lines without re-architecture.
Omnichannel
It lives where the passenger already is —the app and WhatsApp— plus a lightweight physical touchpoint with a QR code at the station. No changes to existing infrastructure.
Real time
It integrates via API with the service-status feeds that operations already has. Information arrives on time and personalized, not as a generic announcement over the loudspeaker.
Built on Hapee
It runs on our proprietary platform, cloud-native on AWS, multi-engine (GPT, Gemini and Claude) and with native MCP connection to Anthropic's Claude.

What advancing to the next phase means

Metro's Demo Day is a staged process, and advancing means something very concrete: the proposal was reviewed and selected to stay in the race — and with that, we're already in at Metro: this stage includes mentorships and AI development plans alongside the Metro team, working on the project from inside the network.

The grand winner will be announced at the end of the year. And while that final approaches, the relationship is already in motion: we're exploring side projects with Metro in parallel with the competition. Whatever the December result, Vía is already running on rails.

Why we're going in with confidence

Vía isn't a lab experiment or an idea on a slide: it runs on a platform that's already in production with real customers. The pilot's challenge isn't inventing the technology, it's adapting it to the operator.
