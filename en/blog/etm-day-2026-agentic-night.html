See you at EtM Day: Hapee booth and Agentic Night

From Thursday, November 19 to Saturday, November 21 we’ll have a booth at EtM Day 2026. And on Friday the 20th, when the day wraps up, join us at Agentic Night, the Hapee x ACHIAG after-party.

Nov 4, 2026 Events · EtM Day 3 min read By Hapee Team
In brief

Hapee booth at EtM Day: Thursday, November 19 to Saturday, November 21.

Agentic Night: Friday, November 20, 7:00 to 10:00 p.m., at Vinolia (Vitacura). Limited spots, registration on Luma.

Visit us at the booth

Stop by the Hapee booth to meet our AI agents —the ones that chat on WhatsApp and by voice, book appointments and follow up on their own— and talk with the team about bringing them to your business.

ACHIAG, the Chilean Association of Agentic Artificial Intelligence chaired by our CEO, Sebastián Figueroa, will also be there as an official 2026 partner of EtM Day.

Agentic Night: the agentic conversation continues

“EtMday is over, but the agentic conversation continues.” Agentic Night | After EtMday by Hapee x ACHIAG is a night to connect with founders, teams and leaders who are building AI agents in Latin America.

When
Friday, November 20, 7:00 to 10:00 p.m.
Where
Vinolia, Alonso de Monroy 2869, Vitacura (Santiago), a short walk from Parque Bicentenario.
Part of
Chile Tech Week 2026.
Spots
Limited, with prior registration on Luma.
