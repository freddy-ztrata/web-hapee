Automate your social content with Hapee

Schedule, publish, measure and reply on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and WhatsApp from a single dashboard. Your AI agents turn every comment and DM into an opportunity. This is the step-by-step guide, with real numbers from LATAM customers.

May 1, 2026 How-to · Marketing 9 min read By Hapee Team

Anyone who has run a business's social media knows that publishing is the easy part. The hard part comes after: the 200 DMs a day that campaigns generate, the comments piling up across 6 different platforms, the lead who asks "how much does it cost?" on a Friday at 11:47 PM and goes cold until Monday. That is where most businesses lose sales they had already won.

Hapee solves this with four modules that live on the same platform: Social Planner (planning + publishing), AI Content (content generation), Conversations (unified omnichannel inbox) and Workflows (trigger-based automation). This guide shows you how they connect and what they can do together.

TL;DR

You connect Instagram, FB, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, GMB and WhatsApp to the Hapee dashboard. You generate content with AI using Claude as the engine (the same model as your conversational agent). Every comment or DM can trigger a workflow that qualifies, tags and assigns automatically. Two-way webhooks connect everything to your external stack. Your team only replies to leads that are already qualified.

The real problem

If a community manager spends 4 hours a day on social media, in a typical business that time breaks down like this:

  • 1 h designing and building the post (Canva, captions, hashtags)
  • 30 min scheduling it in Buffer/Metricool, copying the same text adapted to each network
  • 2 h replying to DMs and comments across 6 different inboxes, pasting the same message 30 times
  • 30 min putting together the weekly report by hand, collecting screenshots from each platform

80% of the time goes to operational work, not creative work. Meanwhile, unanswered DMs lose value with every hour they sit in the queue. Meta ads studies show that after 30 minutes without a reply, the chance of closing drops by half.

What is Social Planner

Social Planner is the social media management module inside the suite. It has four main views:

Calendar view
Monthly/weekly drag-and-drop grid with every scheduled post across all networks. Move a post to another day by dragging it. Color-coded by social network. List view for the details.
Composer
Multi-network editor in a single view. You upload an image/video, write a base caption and AI Content generates the variant adapted to each platform (IG length ≠ LinkedIn length ≠ TikTok hook).
Library
Reusable content library. Posts that worked, templates, hashtags by pillar, brand assets. Mark a post as "evergreen" and it goes out again every 60 days.
Analytics
Real-time metrics by network, post, hashtag and content pillar. End-to-end attribution: post → DM → lead → close. Not just "likes", but the real sales tied to them.

Connect your accounts to the dashboard · the step that changes everything

The real "aha" moment

Most businesses run their social media with 6 tabs open in parallel: Meta Business Suite, TikTok Studio, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, X, YouTube, GMB. Connecting everything to Hapee isn't "having yet another dashboard". It's a paradigm shift: a single operation that affects all 6 networks, instead of 6 repeated operations.

One-click OAuth: go to Settings → Integrations → Social and authorize each account with a button. Once an account lives inside the dashboard, everything that comes next — AI, scheduling, workflows, unified inbox — works on top of that connection. That's why this is the cornerstone and the most important step.

Channels you connect to Hapee:

  • Instagram (Business / Creator) — posts, reels, stories, carousels
  • Facebook (Pages) — posts, stories, reels
  • TikTok Business — vertical videos
  • LinkedIn (Page + Personal) — text posts, articles, documents
  • X / Twitter — posts + threads
  • YouTube — shorts + full uploads
  • Google Business Profile — GMB posts for local SEO
  • WhatsApp Business API — broadcasts with approved templates

Each account is assigned to a workspace. If you manage several brands or locations (typical for franchises), each one has its own permissions and reports. One authorization, all your publishing from the same place.

Content generation · a direct bridge to Claude

Once your accounts are connected to the dashboard, AI Content runs on top of that data. And the engine behind it isn't a generic LLM: it's the same Claude (via native MCP) that answers your customers in the conversational agent. The tone you already validated in your public chat carries over identically to organic content, without training two different systems.

AI captions

Hashtags
With one click the AI suggests hashtags relevant to the caption and to the channel where it will be published.
Variants per channel
The same concept rewritten to respect each network's format and length, without you having to redo it by hand.
Image generation
Integration with an AI generator to accompany your posts when you don't have your own asset ready.

Webhooks and API: integration with your stack

This is where Hapee stops being "just another tool" and becomes infrastructure. Every social action (post, comment, DM, mention) can be triggered or captured via webhook, connecting Hapee with the rest of your operation:

  • Hapee → your stack · when something happens on social media, Hapee notifies your system. E.g.: comment qualified as interested → POST to your CRM, Google Sheet, Slack or in-house system.
  • Your stack → Hapee · you trigger actions from your system. E.g.: a contract is signed on your platform → Hapee publishes a "new customer" post on LinkedIn.
  • Claude as a direct bridge · via the MCP integration, your conversational AI agent can generate and publish content from a conversation. "Hey, post a story with the discount we closed with María" and the agent builds the caption, suggests the asset and schedules it. Without going through the dashboard.

The secret sauce: workflows that reply for you

This is where Hapee sets itself apart from any scheduling tool. Every social interaction can trigger a workflow.

Available triggers

  • New comment on an IG / FB / LinkedIn post
  • Mention in an Instagram story
  • New DM on any network
  • WhatsApp message with a specific keyword
  • Comment that matches a pattern (regex, keyword, sentiment)
  • New reaction on a post

Actions inside the workflow

  • Reply with a message (fixed text or AI-generated in your voice)
  • Send a DM with a link, a calendar or a PDF
  • Create/update a contact in the CRM with tags by channel and by pillar
  • Assign to a salesperson based on rules (language, time, tag, estimated amount)
  • Trigger a nurturing email/SMS
  • Notify the team on Slack/Discord/Teams
  • Wait X minutes, add a condition, do A or B
A concrete example

A customer comments "price?" on a Reel. Workflow: Hapee replies with a friendly DM ("Hi! Here are the details"), sends a link to the pricing calculator, tags them as interested-price, assigns them to the salesperson on shift, and if they haven't replied in 30 minutes it sends a follow-up. All automatic. The salesperson sees the lead in their pipeline already qualified.

Conversations: 1 queue, all channels

DMs that are not answered automatically land in Conversations: a single unified inbox with conversations from IG, FB Messenger, WhatsApp, X DMs, LinkedIn, SMS, email and web chat.

Assignment

What changes when everything lives in the dashboard

You notice the real operational change in three concrete things — not in "likes":

  • Response time drops substantially. DMs and comments stop sitting in the queue because workflows answer the frequent ones automatically and the unified inbox groups the ones that need a human.
  • DM → sale conversion goes up. The lead reaches the salesperson already tagged by channel, pillar and intent. The salesperson steps in with context instead of asking the basics.
  • Unanswered comments trend toward zero. The workflow auto-replies to the frequent ones ("price?", "do you ship?", "in stock?") and frees up the team for the truly conversational ones.

The exact numbers vary by industry and business volume, but the pattern repeats consistently: less manual work, more qualified leads without extra effort from the team.

How to get started in 24 hours

Anyone with access to the dashboard can get it up and running on day one. Here is the exact sequence:

  1. Connect channels (30 min) Go to Settings → Integrations → Social and authorize each account. Check that the last 30 days of posts show up imported.
  2. Set up your brand voice (15 min) Describe how you want to sound in 1-2 paragraphs (formal/casual, ironic/professional, approachable/corporate) and a few key rules (which words to avoid, how to close posts). The AI uses that as the base for all generation.
  3. Load 2 weeks of content (1 h) Import a CSV or paste 10 captions into the composer. Hapee generates variants per channel and places them on the calendar at the best-time-to-post slots.
  4. Set up 3 base workflows (45 min) (a) comment with "price/cost" → DM with calculator · (b) DM with "demo" → booking calendar · (c) story mention → thank-you DM + coupon.
  5. Turn the inbox on (15 min) Assign your salespeople, set a 5-minute SLA, enable AI Assist. The first message that arrives already goes through the whole flow.
  6. Daily email report (5 min) Enable the digest: every morning at 8:00 AM you get yesterday's summary with closing numbers and the top 3 posts.

Total: ~3 hours of setup, and it keeps running 24/7.
