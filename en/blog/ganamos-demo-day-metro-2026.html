We won Metro Demo Day 2026 with Vía by Hapee

Santiago Metro selected Vía by Hapee among the winners of Metro Demo Day 2026. Our AI travel assistant for passengers, built for service disruptions, went from application to mentoring, from mentoring to pitch, and from pitch to the awards stage.

Nov 23, 2026 News · Vía by Hapee 4 min read By Hapee Team
The news in short

On Monday, November 23, Metro’s Innovation team wrote to us: our proposal “stood out among the initiatives presented” and was “selected as a winner of this edition”.

Vía by Hapee competed in challenge 3.1, “Integrated platform for customer experience and disruption management”. Metro will share the next steps soon.

What Vía by Hapee is

Vía by Hapee is an AI travel assistant that accompanies Metro passengers in real time, with a focus on service disruptions: a stopped train, a detour, a change in service. Right when support channels get overloaded, Vía answers in the channel where the passenger already is.

One brain
Not one agent per station: a single engine that serves the whole network and can grow to new lines without re-architecting.
Omnichannel
The app and WhatsApp, plus a light physical QR touchpoint in stations, without touching existing infrastructure.
Real time
Connects by API to the service status feeds the operation already has.
Built on Hapee
Runs on our own platform, cloud-native on AWS and multi-engine (GPT, Gemini and Claude).

What changed during mentoring

Between the application and Pitch Day there were five mentoring sessions with Metro teams. In the third one, focused on adjusting the value proposition, we learned how a disruption works today: the control center detects the event and a communications lead approves before anything is published.

That changed our focus. Instead of adding one more channel, Vía concentrates that approval into a single action, and from there the message goes out consistently to every channel. It was the key slide of the pitch:

30 → 1
One approval
From more than 30 posts to a single approval.
1 → 4
One message per moment
From one message for everyone to one for each moment of the trip.

Everyone gains something: passengers know what is happening, how long it will last and what alternative they have; communications approves once and its work is multiplied, not replaced; and Metro delivers a consistent message across all its channels.

Why it is feasible

Vía does not start from scratch: it runs on Hapee, which is already in production with real customers. That is why the proposal considers a limited pilot of about four months in high-traffic stations.

What comes next with Metro

Metro will soon share the next steps and the coordination for this new stage. Our answer was short: we are all in.

Meanwhile, you can explore Vía’s public demo at hapee.ai/via, including a walkable 3D station.
