How Vía works

One assistant for the whole network, on every channel

  1. 01

    One brain

    It is not one agent per station: it is a single engine that serves the entire network and scales to future lines without re-architecture.

  2. 02

    Omnichannel

    It lives where riders already are —the app and WhatsApp— plus a lightweight physical QR point in the station. No changes to existing infrastructure.

  3. 03

    Real time

    It integrates via API with the service status feeds the operation already has. Information arrives on time and personalized, not as a generic announcement over the PA.

No changes to existing infrastructure.

Vía builds on what already works: the channels riders use every day and the status feeds the operation already has.

A trip with Vía

From the mezzanine to the platform, with Vía by your side

This is the route you can take today in the 3D station: you go from Universidad de Chile to Baquedano on Line 1.

  1. 1

    You scan the QR

    At the Vía Point on the mezzanine, the robot invites you to scan the code and chat.

  2. 2

    It tells you where to go

    How do I get to Baquedano? Platform 2, toward Los Dominicos: it's 3 stops.

  3. 3

    You board the train

    On the platform, Vía confirms the train's direction and the next station before you board.

  4. 4

    You ask how many stops are left

    The same chat stays with you on the train: You're on track: Baquedano is 3 stops away.

  5. 5

    It alerts you before you arrive

    We're arriving at Santa Lucía. Baquedano is 2 stops away.

About Hapee

Vía runs on Hapee

Vía is not a lab experiment or an idea on a slide: it runs on a platform that is already in production with real customers. The pilot's challenge is not inventing the technology, it is adapting it to the operator.

  • Proprietary platform
  • Cloud-native on AWS
  • Multi-engine: GPT, Gemini and Claude
  • Native MCP with Anthropic's Claude
  • +2KAutomated customersThe Hapee platform runs today with real customers, in production.
  • ~2.4 sAgent response timeSpeed that matters when thousands of riders ask at once.
  • 24/7In every disruptionAutomatic scaling on AWS: it grows at peak and returns to baseline.

The proposal starts with a limited pilot at high-traffic stations: low risk, fast learning.
