01 · The problem

The critical moment isn't the trip: it's the disruption

On a network that moves millions of people every day, when a train is stopped, a route is diverted or service changes, customer service channels collapse and passenger uncertainty skyrockets. The information exists, but it doesn't reach the people who need it on time, personalized, in the channel where they need it.

02 · The solution

A single brain that scales across the whole network

Vía by Hapee accompanies the passenger in real time, with a special focus on service disruptions. It is not one agent per station: it is a single engine that serves the whole network. It lives where the passenger already is — the app and WhatsApp — and relies on a lightweight physical point in the station (QR touchpoint via Linkd), without touching the existing infrastructure.

Single, scalable agent

The same engine serves the entire network and grows to future lines (7, 8, 9) with no re-architecture.

Omnichannel

App, WhatsApp and a physical QR point in the station, with a consistent experience across all of them.

Contextual and real-time

Integrates via API with the service-status feeds the operation already has, with no structural changes.

Living knowledge base

Updatable online and managed by the operator itself: the information is always up to date.

Multi-engine architecture

Runs on several AI engines (GPT, Gemini and Claude/Anthropic), choosing the best one for each task.

Voice and audio

Voice agents and audio understanding (official ElevenLabs partnership) to broaden accessibility.

03 · Architecture

Built on Hapee, cloud-native on AWS

A proprietary platform built entirely on AWS, designed from scratch to operate at scale. It is not legacy software or an assembly of third-party pieces.

  • Automatic scaling. During a disruption, when thousands of passengers ask at once, the infrastructure grows in seconds and returns to its baseline when the peak passes — with no outages from overload.
  • Modern stack. React frontend with a visual flow builder; decoupled serverless backend; API integrations with the operator's systems.
  • Native MCP connection with Anthropic's Claude — a capability unique in LATAM — that lets the agent work with tools and context in a secure, structured way.
  • Security and compliance. Encryption in transit and at rest, traceability and auditing of every interaction, and a design compliant with Chile's Law 21.719 on data protection.
04 · Why it's feasible

Not a lab experiment

Vía by Hapee is not built from scratch: it runs on Hapee, a proprietary platform that is already in production with real customers. The pilot is not about inventing the technology; it is about adapting it to the operator.

Direct proof · Sacyr case

Today we run, for Sacyr, an infrastructure concessionaire, an agent that handles user inquiries 24/7 and routes them to the right team on its own. It is practically the same problem, already solved and working in the real world — which means low implementation risk and a scoped pilot of ~4 months at high-traffic stations.

05 · What each side gains

Value for the passenger and the operator

The passenger

  • Less uncertainty in critical moments.
  • Personalized information in their own channel.
  • Better accessibility (text, voice and audio).

The operator

  • Offloads its service channels right when they are most overloaded.
  • Better management of every disruption.
  • Clear, actionable data on what passengers actually ask about.
