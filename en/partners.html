Frequently asked questions

Everything people ask before applying.

What are the requirements to apply?
At least 3 active customers, a focus on digital marketing / sales / automation, and passing a 30-minute interview. No minimum revenue or required portfolio.
How much does it cost to join?
Nothing. Partner, Pro and Elite have no entry fee. Higher tiers are earned through volume, not bought.
How does the recurring commission work?
Every customer you close stays linked to your account for life. You earn 20 / 28 / 35% of the customer's net MRR for as long as they keep paying. Every month, not just the first.
Does Hapee send me leads, or do I find my own?
Both. You generate most of them through your own marketing (we give you the co-branded kit). Pro partners receive warm leads from the public directory and Elite partners join the priority enterprise pool.
Which countries does it cover?
All of LATAM (12 countries) + Spain. Payments in USD or local currency depending on the country. Other regions are evaluated case by case.
